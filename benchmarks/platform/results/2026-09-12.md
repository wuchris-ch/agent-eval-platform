## Corrections found during release verification

Concurrent permission checks could change evidence ctime unnecessarily, and SQLite could remove a sidecar between its existence check and open. Both cases now have deterministic regression tests. Atomic writes finish permission/ACL setup before publishing the inode. Existing symlink rejection remains tested. The report generator also requires all three trials for every corpus case and the stated score/grade thresholds before issuing a release PASS.

Real-provider OIDC and a representative held-out evaluation corpus remain outside this verification. Live reviewer quality is recorded separately in the README and its linked benchmark report.
